PROFILE

Hi, I'm Etika.

I am a Data Engineer and Analytics Developer with a Bachelor's degree in Informatics and professional experience in data and information system development.

Currently, I work with the Forest Program at the Wildlife Conservation Society (WCS), where I develop data integration workflows, manage relational and spatial databases, and build analytical applications to support conservation programs.

My work spans the data lifecycle — from data extraction and transformation to database management, validation, and visualization. I am particularly interested in data architecture, data governance, and scalable information systems that bridge the gap between raw data and meaningful insights.
